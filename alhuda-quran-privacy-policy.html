1. Information We Collect

No Personal Data Collection: This App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

Local Data Storage

  • Notes: Notes created within the App are stored locally on your device using device storage (SharedPreferences). We do not have access to your notes.
  • Preferences: App settings and user preferences are stored locally on your device.

All data remains on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

2. Audio and Documents

The App includes Quran audio and PDF content bundled within the application. These files are provided as part of the App functionality and do not collect or transmit user data.

3. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Audio Playback: To play Quran recitations within the App.
  • Background Audio: To allow playback when the App is minimized or the device is locked.
  • Media Playback Notification: When audio is playing, Android may display a system notification with playback controls (play, pause, stop). This notification is required for background media playback and does not collect or transmit any user data.

These permissions are used strictly for core media playback functionality and not for data collection or messaging.

4. Third-Party Services

This App does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or external data collection tools.

5. Children's Privacy

This App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, the App is safe for general audiences.

6. Data Security

All user data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device’s built-in security features.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App’s data from your device settings.
