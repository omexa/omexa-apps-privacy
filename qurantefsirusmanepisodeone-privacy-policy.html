Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Omexa Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles user privacy for the mobile application ቁርአን ተፍሲር በኡስታዝ ኡስማን (Quran Tefsir Usman) (package name: com.omexa.qurantefsirusmanepsode01).

Zero Data Collection Guarantee:
We respect your privacy. This application does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user identifiers, or device information. We do NOT collect the Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, IP address, contacts, names, or location. The app is completely ad-free, tracking-free, and operates strictly on your local device.

1. No Personal Data or Identifier Collection

We believe in complete user privacy. When you download, install, and use this application:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or name to use the app.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do NOT collect or read any Advertising ID (AAID / GAID), Android ID, IMEI, or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Tracking / No Profiling: We do NOT track your usage, behavior, or app interactions across third-party apps or websites.
  • No Analytics or Crash Tracking: We do NOT use third-party analytics or behavioral tracking services.

2. Strictly Local On-Device Storage

All application features operate entirely offline and store data locally on your device's private sandbox storage:

  • Tefsir Audio Lessons: Audio lessons are bundled directly within the app or downloaded locally to your device storage. Playback progress and history are kept locally via AsyncStorage and are never transmitted to any external server.
  • Quran Mushaf & Database: The Holy Quran text and Surah information are stored in an offline local SQLite database on your device.
  • User Notes & Bookmarks: Any notes you take or pages you bookmark are saved solely in your device's private storage. They never leave your phone.
  • Preferences: Display settings (such as dark mode, text size, and audio volume) are saved locally on your phone.
  • Audio Sharing: If you use the native audio share feature to share an audio lesson with others, it uses Android's native system sharing sheet (expo-sharing). No user data is logged or uploaded during this process.

3. Advertising Policy: No Ads & No Ad Tracking

This application contains NO active advertisements.

  • No banner ads, interstitial popups, or video ads are displayed.
  • The Google Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) has been explicitly removed from the application.
  • No user data or advertising identifiers are shared with any advertising network or third-party marketing agency.

4. Android Permissions Disclosures

The application requests only the minimum essential Android permissions necessary to provide Islamic Tefsir audio playback and reading:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required solely to allow uninterrupted background audio playback of Quran Tefsir lessons when switching apps or locking the device screen.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ solely to display media playback controls (Play, Pause, Previous, Next) in the notification bar.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Standard network permissions used to check network availability. No personal data or user identifiers are transmitted.

These permissions are never used to record private audio, track location, or access device files outside the app's sandboxed storage.

5. Children's Privacy & Families Policy

Our application is an educational and religious Quran Tefsir learning resource designed for a general audience of all ages. Because the application collects zero personal information, uses no user identifiers, and serves no advertisements, it is completely safe for children and fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

6. Data Retention, Account Deletion & Clearing Data

Because we do not collect personal data or maintain user accounts on remote cloud servers, there is no online profile or cloud account to delete.

You have total control over all local on-device data. You can delete all local notes, bookmarks, and preferences at any time by:

  • Going to Settings > Apps > ቁርአን ተፍሲር በኡስታዝ ኡስማን > Storage > tapping Clear Data, OR
  • Uninstalling the application from your Android device.

7. Security

We take user privacy and security seriously. All local application files are confined to the secure application sandbox provided by the Android operating system, preventing unauthorized access by other apps.
