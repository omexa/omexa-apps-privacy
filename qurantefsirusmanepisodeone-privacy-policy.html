Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Omexa Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information for the mobile application ቁርአን ተፍሲር በኡስታዝ ኡስማን (Quran Tefsir Usman) (package name: com.omexa.qurantefsirusmanepsode01).

Summary for Google Play Console & Users:
We respect your privacy. Our app does not collect personal identity information (like names, emails, phone numbers, or contacts). We use trusted third-party advertising services (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and Meta Audience Network) to display non-intrusive advertisements.

1. Data Collection & Usage

A. Personal Data

We do not collect, store, or request any personally identifiable information (PII) directly within the application.

B. Local On-Device Data Storage

  • Notes, Bookmarks & Audio Progress: Notes written by users, saved PDF bookmarks, and audio playback positions/history are stored strictly on your local device using AsyncStorage and local SQLite database. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any external servers.
  • User Preferences: App preferences (such as dark mode, font size, and reader orientation) remain strictly on your local device storage.
  • Audio Sharing: When you use the optional single-audio share feature, local MP3 files are shared directly via native system share dialogs (using expo-sharing). No user data is logged or uploaded during this process.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Data Safety Disclosures

This application integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to serve advertisements (Banners, Interstitial Ads, and Rewarded Video Ads). These third-party ad networks may automatically collect non-personal device information to serve relevant ads, prevent ad fraud, and provide analytics:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): Used for frequency capping, ad measurement, and detecting invalid click activity.
  • Device Technical Information: Hardware model, OS version, language setting, screen orientation, and network connection type.
  • General Location Data: Country-level or coarse IP location to serve regionally relevant ads.
  • Ad Performance Metrics: Ad view duration, completion rates, and user interactions with ads.

Advertising Network Partners:

Please review the privacy policies of our third-party ad partners for more details on how they process data:

  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Advertising Choices & Opt-Out (Google Advertising ID)

Users can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time directly through their Android device settings:

  • Open your device Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Tap Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. Android Permissions Disclosures

To provide core Islamic Tefsir media playback and reading, the application requests the following Android permissions:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to maintain uninterrupted Tefsir audio lesson playback when switching apps or locking your phone screen.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display system media playback controls (Play, Pause, Skip) in the Android notification shade.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to check network connectivity and load ad impressions from ad providers.

These permissions are used strictly for media playback and ad serving. No audio, voice, or private data is ever recorded or transmitted.

5. Families Policy & Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for a general audience (all ages) for educational and religious study. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. All ad networks integrated into the app are configured to comply with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA regulations.

6. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

Since we do not collect personal user accounts or store user data on external cloud servers, there is no remote user account to delete.

You can permanently remove all local notes, bookmarks, preferences, and app cache at any time by:

  • Going to Settings > Apps > ቁርአን ተፍሲር በኡስታዝ ኡስማን > Storage > tap Clear Data, OR
  • Uninstalling the mobile application from your device.

7. Security

We value your trust in using our app. All local user data is stored inside the app's protected sandbox directory on your device, safeguarded by Android OS security mechanisms.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
